Study & Focus Tips

  • 1. Lock Your Schedule Keep consistent bedtimes and study slots every single day to maximize your natural focus windows.

  • 3. Active Self-Testing Instead of just re-reading text, quiz yourself on core formulas or definitions to anchor them securely into long-term memory.
